Glasses Hub support

Glasses Hub shows scores, news, weather, tools and games on RayNeo iO smart glasses. It is an independent app and is not made or endorsed by RayNeo.

Getting started

  1. Pair your glasses in the RayNeo app and keep that app installed. Glasses Hub shares its connection.
  2. In the RayNeo app's settings for your glasses, set the crown double-click shortcut to None.
  3. Open Glasses Hub and tap Start display. Allow Bluetooth when asked.

Controls on the glasses

  • Turn the crown forward: next item or page
  • Turn the crown back: previous item or page
  • Look up briefly: select or open
  • Double click the crown: apps menu
  • Hold the crown: leave the page

Settings > Try the controls walks you through each one.

Common questions

The display won't start. Check the glasses are connected in the RayNeo app, close anything open on the glasses, and try again.

Double click opens the RayNeo assistant. Set the double-click shortcut to None in the RayNeo app.

Phone notifications stopped showing on the glasses. The glasses don't show them while Glasses Hub is on screen. Use "Pop up only when something comes in" on the Display tab, or the dashboard's option to let notifications through while blank.

Text is cut off at the top or wraps early. Lower the characters per line under Settings > Screen and text.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/jarredksmith/glasses-hub/issues. Including the connection log (Settings > Connection log > Copy) helps a lot.
