Glasses Hub privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Glasses Hub is an iPhone app that shows text on RayNeo iO smart glasses. It is made by Jarred Smith, an independent developer. It is not made or endorsed by RayNeo.

The short version

  • Glasses Hub has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
  • The developer does not collect, receive or store any information about you or how you use the app.
  • Your settings and anything you type stay on your iPhone. Access keys and tokens are kept in the iPhone's Keychain.
  • Some features fetch information from outside services, and a few optional ones send those services something of yours. Those are listed below. Each is off until you use that feature.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Bluetooth. The app talks to your glasses over Bluetooth to show text and to read the crown and head movements. Nothing from the glasses is sent anywhere else.
  • Calendar. If you allow it, the app reads today's events to show them on the glasses. They are not sent anywhere.
  • Microphone, for the tuner and the guitar and drum trainers. They listen for notes and drum hits on the phone. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Text you paste or send in. Reader text, messages, and text sent by Shortcuts or other apps through the local address stay on the phone.

Features that contact an outside service

These requests go straight from your iPhone to the service named. Like any internet request, the service can see your IP address. The developer never sees these requests.

Location

Location is used only by the walking tour and planes overhead. It is read while one of them is showing. If you turn on the walking tour's "Tell me when I pass something", location keeps being read while the display is on, including with the phone locked. It is sent only to the services in the table above, and is not stored.

Health-related data

If you add a data feed for a glucose reading or similar, that value is fetched from the address you entered and shown on your glasses. It is not stored beyond the latest reading and is not sent anywhere. Glasses Hub is not a medical device and should not be used for treatment decisions.

Children

Glasses Hub is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. There is nothing held by the developer to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
